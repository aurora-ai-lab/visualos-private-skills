# Fashion Sketch Portrait

Category: **VisualOS**

## Purpose

Redraw a person's photo as a high-fashion semi-realistic hand-drawn portrait in coloured pencil, light watercolour and fashion-sketch line work — keeping the original face, hair, clothing colours and props intact on a near-white paper ground.

## Invocation

Installed skill name: `fashion-sketch-portrait`.

```text
$fashion-sketch-portrait
以这张照片作为唯一人物参考和唯一造型参考，画成高级半写实时尚手绘肖像，严格保留脸型、发型、服装原色和眼镜，3:4 竖版。
```
